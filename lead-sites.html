What is a Lead Site?

Simple: it's a website that works like a billboard, a business card, and a front desk — pointed at your phone.

🎯 One trade, one town

Each site is built around a single trade in a single town — "towing in Valdosta," "AC repair in Brunswick" — the exact words your next customer types into Google.

🔎 Built to be found

We write it, structure it, and tune it for local search, then keep working it so it climbs — and stays — in the results.

📞 Calls go to you

The number on the site rings your phone. Quote requests land in your email. Nothing passes through a call center, and we never take a cut of a job.

🔐 Exclusive by design

One business rents each trade in each town. While you rent it, your competitor across town can't — first come, first served.

🛠 We maintain everything

Hosting, security, updates, and the ranking work are all on us. Your only job is answering the phone and doing good work.

💰 One flat rent

No per-lead fees, no percentage of your jobs, no contract lock-in. A busy month costs you the same as a slow one.

Straight answers

The questions every shop owner asks us about Lead Sites.

Do you guarantee a certain number of calls?

No — and you should run from anyone who does. Call volume depends on how many people in your town search for your trade, and nobody controls that. What we control is building the site right and ranking it. Your rent is flat either way: a big month costs you exactly the same as a quiet one, and you'll never get a per-lead bill from us.

Can my competitor rent the same trade in my town?

No. One renter per trade, per town — that's the whole value of it. If your trade in your town is already taken, we'll tell you plainly and, if you like, flag nearby towns that are still open.

Do I own the website?

No — that's exactly what keeps the price where it is. Ziteo owns the site and the domain; you rent the calls and quote requests it produces. If you'd rather own a site outright, that's our Ownership plan — a different product, and we're happy to talk through which fits you better.

How fast will the phone start ringing?

Honestly: ranking a brand-new site takes time — weeks to months, not days. Before you commit, we'll look at the real search demand for your trade in your town and show you what we see. We won't rent you a site we don't believe can rank.

What happens if I cancel?

The routing stops at the end of your paid period — calls and quote requests no longer come to you, and the site stays with Ziteo. It may then be offered to another business in your trade and town. No cancellation fees, no hard feelings.

What do I have to do?

Answer the phone and do good work. We handle the site, the hosting, the ranking, and the routing. If you want the site to mention a new service or seasonal special, text us — we update it.
