Questions, answered

How fast can my site be live?

Most sites go live within a few days of our first call. You give us your info and photos — we do everything else.

Do I own my domain name?

Yes. Your domain (like yourshop.com) is registered in your name. We just point it at your site and manage the technical side.

What happens if I cancel the rental plan?

Straight answer: the site goes offline 30 days after your last payment, and the files stay with us — that's what makes the rental price possible. If you think you might want out later, the ownership plan is the better fit.

Can I switch from rental to ownership?

Yes. After 12 months of rental payments, you can buy out the site for a flat conversion fee and own it outright.

Do you do online stores or advertising?

Online stores are quoted per project — tell us what you sell. Advertising management is coming soon as an add-on for site customers.

I'm not technical at all. Is this for me?

That's exactly who it's for. You will never log into a dashboard, install an update, or touch code. Text us what you want changed — done.
