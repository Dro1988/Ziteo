Wix vs Hiring a Web Designer: The Real Math

October 2026 · 4 min read

"Just build it on Wix, it's $17 a month" versus "hire a designer for $2,000." Both sound simple. Neither price tells the whole story. Let's do the actual math.

What Wix really costs

The subscription is the smallest part. The real costs:

  • Your build time. Expect the better part of a weekend to get something you're not embarrassed by — longer if you're writing copy from scratch.
  • Your maintenance time. An hour or two a month: updating hours, swapping photos, fixing the thing that broke after an update.
  • Renewal jumps. Intro pricing is a marketing tool. Budget builders commonly renew at 2–3× the advertised rate.
  • Lock-in. You can't take a Wix site with you. Leave, and you rebuild from zero.

One independent 2026 comparison priced the true DIY cost — subscription plus a modest value on the owner's time — at roughly $50–$80/month across the major builders. The $17 headline is real; it's just not the total.

What a designer really costs

A freelancer charges $500–$2,000 for a template-based small business site, $1,500–$8,000+ for custom work. That's the build. Then:

  • Updates are extra. Most freelancers bill $50–$150/hour for changes after launch. Move to a new shop? New hours? That's a ticket, not a text.
  • Hosting is on you. Another subscription, another login, another thing that can break.
  • Availability varies. Your freelancer has other clients. "Quick change" can mean next week.

The third option nobody mentions

Done-for-you subscription services: someone builds the site and runs it, for a flat monthly fee. UENI does it at $24.99/month; their Plus tier with unlimited edits is $59/month. You get the "hire someone" experience at a DIY-ish price, because the provider spreads the labor across many clients with templates and systems.

Which should you pick?

  • Pick DIY if you enjoy tinkering, have more time than money, and your site is simple.
  • Pick a freelancer if you want to own everything outright and have $1,000+ to spend.
  • Pick a done-for-you subscription if you want it handled — one price, no tech, changes done for you.
